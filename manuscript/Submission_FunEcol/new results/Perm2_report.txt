INTERPRETATION

1. EFFECT-SIZE SELECTION IS REAL AND SUBSTANTIAL.
   The stratified null shifts from Δρ ≈ 0.00 (naive) to Δρ ≈ 0.22
   (stratified), confirming that ~60% of the raw Δρ is attributable
   to the fact that shared DAPs were selected for large fold-changes.
   The QED review (Gap 4.1) was correct to flag this.

2. A SIGNIFICANT RESIDUAL SIGNAL PERSISTS.
   Even after magnitude and detectability matching, the observed
   Δρ = 0.36 exceeds ALL 2,000 permuted values (max null ≈ 0.33).
   The "true" constraint signal (observed − null mean ≈ 0.14) is
   smaller than the raw Δρ but remains highly significant.

3. THE "EVOLUTIONARY CONSTRAINT" CLAIM IS DEFENSIBLE, BUT NEEDS
   RECALIBRATION.
   The original MS implies Δρ = 0.35 is entirely due to constraint.
   The corrected interpretation: ~60% reflects effect-size selection,
   ~40% (Δρ ≈ 0.14) reflects genuine excess concordance among
   shared DAPs beyond what magnitude alone predicts.
